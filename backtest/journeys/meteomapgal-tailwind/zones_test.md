# Switch between the two zones

## Open the map
If a welcome tour is shown, click "Saltar" to close it.

## Switch to the Rías zone
Click the "Rías" button at the top left of the map.
Verify the header shows "Rías Baixas".

## Switch back to the interior zone
Click the "Interior" button at the top left of the map.
Verify the header shows "Interior sur de Galicia".
