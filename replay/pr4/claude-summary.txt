The checkout form crashed on the zod 4 upgrade when you submitted it. Zod 4 removed the `ZodError.errors` alias, so `result.error.errors.forEach(...)` in `src/pages/Checkout.jsx` hit `undefined` (the "reading 'forEach'" console error), and no validation messages were ever shown. I changed that one line to `result.error.issues.forEach(...)`, which is the property zod 4 uses. `npm run build` succeeds, but I couldn't run the quick script that checks zod 4 returns both messages because it needed an approval I didn't have, and I didn't replay the journey in a browser.
