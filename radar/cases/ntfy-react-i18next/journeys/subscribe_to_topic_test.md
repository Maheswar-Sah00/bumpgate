# Subscribe to a topic from the home page and open it

## Open the subscribe dialog
On the home page, click the "Subscribe to topic" button in the side navigation.
Verify a dialog titled "Subscribe to topic" is shown with a "Topic name, e.g. phil_alerts" field.

## Enter a topic name and subscribe
Type "kane_alerts_demo" into the "Topic name, e.g. phil_alerts" field.
Click the "Subscribe" button in the dialog.

## Check the new subscription
Verify the dialog closes and "kane_alerts_demo" appears in the side navigation under "Subscribed topics".
Verify the page shows the text "You haven't received any notifications for this topic yet."
