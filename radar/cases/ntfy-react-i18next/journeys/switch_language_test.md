# Switch the app language to German and back to English

## Open the settings
On the home page, click "Settings" in the side navigation.
Verify the page shows the section headings "Notifications" and "Appearance".

## Choose German
In the "Appearance" section, open the "Language" dropdown and choose "Deutsch".
Verify the section heading "Darstellung" is shown and the side navigation button reads "Thema abonnieren".

## Switch back to English
In the "Darstellung" section, open the "Sprache" dropdown and choose "English".
Verify the section heading "Appearance" is shown and the side navigation button reads "Subscribe to topic".
