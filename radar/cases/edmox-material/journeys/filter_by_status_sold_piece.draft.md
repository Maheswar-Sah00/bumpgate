# Filter the archive by status and see that a sold-out piece cannot be inquired about

## Filter to available pieces
On the home page, scroll to the archive section titled "Fuera de serie".
In the status filter buttons, click "Disponible".
Verify the piece counter reads "04 piezas" (it may be shown in capitals) and a card titled "TAMALUCHI" is visible.
Verify no card titled "GOLDITOS" is shown.

## Filter to sold-out pieces
Click the "Agotado" filter button.
Verify the piece counter reads "10 piezas" and a card titled "GOLDITOS" with the subtitle "Serie kawaii" is visible.

## Open a sold-out piece
Click the card titled "GOLDITOS" with the subtitle "Serie kawaii".
Verify the page shows the heading "GOLDITOS" and the status "Agotado".

## Check the sold-out message
Verify the text "Tiraje agotado. Síguenos en Instagram para enterarte de la siguiente edición." is visible.
Verify there is no "Preguntar por esta pieza" button on the page.
