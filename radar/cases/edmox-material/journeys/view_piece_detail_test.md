# Browse the archive and open a piece's detail page

## Go to the archive
On the home page, click the "Ver el archivo" button in the hero.
Verify the archive section with the heading "Fuera de serie" is visible.

## Open a piece
In the archive grid, click the card titled "CHUPALINAS" with the subtitle "Edición GID" (catalog number "Nº 001").

## Check the piece details
Verify the page shows the large heading "CHUPALINAS" and the subtitle "Edición GID".
Verify the description starting with "Versión GID del kaiju original Chupalinas" is visible.
Verify the details list shows "PLA pintado" as the material, "14 cm" as the height and "Ed. de 30" as the edition.
Verify a "Preguntar por esta pieza" button is visible (do not click it).

## Return to the archive
Click the "← Archivo" link above the photo.
Verify the home page archive with the heading "Fuera de serie" is visible again.
