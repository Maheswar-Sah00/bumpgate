# A user can move between sign-in, password reset and sign-up screens

## Open the password reset screen
From the sign-in screen shown on the home page, click the "Forgot Password?" link.
Verify the screen shows an "Email" field and a "Send Reset Link" button, and no "Login" button.

## Return to sign-in
Click the "Back to Login" link.
Verify the sign-in screen is shown again with the "Login" button.

## Open sign-up and come back
Click the "Create Account!" link.
Verify the "Register" button is visible.
Click the "Sign in now!" link.
Verify the "Login" button and the "Create Account!" link are visible.
