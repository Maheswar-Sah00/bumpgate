# Browse to Bubble Sort, enter custom values and play the sort to the end

## Open the sorting catalogue
On the home page, click the "Browse sorting" button.
Verify the page heading "Sorting" is shown and a card named "Bubble Sort" is listed.

## Open the Bubble Sort visualiser
Click the "Bubble Sort" card.
If a "Welcome to AlgoVerse" guided tour appears, close it with its close (X) button or by pressing Escape.
Verify the heading "Bubble Sort" is shown above the visualiser.

## Enter custom values
Below the visualiser, click the "Custom" button.
In the "Custom values" box, type exactly: 5, 1, 4, 2, 8
Click "Apply".
Verify the text "custom · 5 values" is shown next to the input controls.

## Play the sort to the end
In the playback controls, click the "Jump to end" button (the jump to last step control), or press the End key.
Verify the status text "complete" and the text "Sorted 5 values." are shown below the visualiser.
