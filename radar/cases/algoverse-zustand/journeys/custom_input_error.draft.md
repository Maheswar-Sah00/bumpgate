# Invalid custom values show a clear error and are not applied

## Open Bubble Sort from the catalogue
On the home page, scroll to "Every structure, one engine" and click the "Sorting" category card.
Click the "Bubble Sort" card.
If a "Welcome to AlgoVerse" guided tour appears, close it with its close (X) button or by pressing Escape.
Verify the heading "Bubble Sort" is shown above the visualiser.

## Enter values that are not numbers
Below the visualiser, click the "Custom" button.
In the "Custom values" box, type exactly: five, one, four
Click "Apply".

## Check the error
Verify a notification with the text "Could not parse that input" and "Use comma, space or newline separated integers." is shown, and no "custom ·" values label appears.
