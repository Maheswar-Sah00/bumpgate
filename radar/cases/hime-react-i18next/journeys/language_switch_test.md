# Switch the dashboard to Chinese, keep it after a reload, and switch back to English

## Check the English dashboard
Look at the home page.
Verify the main heading reads "Dashboard" and the subtitle "Real-time live health management" is visible.

## Switch the language to Chinese
In the sidebar, open the "Language" dropdown and choose "中文".
Verify the main heading now reads "仪表盘", the subtitle "实时健康数据管理" is visible, and the sidebar menu shows "技能" and "报告".

## Reload the page
Reload the page.
Verify the subtitle "实时健康数据管理" is still visible, so the chosen language was remembered.

## Switch back to English
In the sidebar language dropdown, choose "English".
Verify the subtitle "Real-time live health management" is visible again and the sidebar menu shows "Skills" and "Reports".
