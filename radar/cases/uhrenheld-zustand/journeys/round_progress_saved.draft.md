# A finished round ends kindly and is saved in the parent view

## Switch off the time limit
Click "Profil" in the bottom navigation.
In the "Einstellungen" section, untick the "Zeitdruck" checkbox.
Click "Karte" in the bottom navigation.

## Start the first station
In the station "Uhr ablesen", click "Stufe 1".
Verify a task with a "Prüfen" button is shown.

## Use up all three hearts with wrong answers
Answer three tasks in a row wrongly, one after another:
- If the question starts with "Wie spät ist es?", type 0 into "Stunde" and 1 into "Minute".
- If the question starts with "Stelle die Uhr auf", click the red "Minutenzeiger" hand on the clock and press the Right Arrow key once.
Then click "Prüfen", check that "Fast! So geht es:" appears, and click "Weiter →".
After the third wrong answer the round ends and a results screen appears.
Verify the results screen shows "Uhr ablesen – Stufe 1", the score "0/6" and the text "Dranbleiben lohnt sich – probier es gleich nochmal!".

## Check the saved progress
Click "Zurück zur Karte".
Click "Eltern" in the bottom navigation.
Verify the "Eltern- & Lehreransicht" shows 1 under "Runden", and the "Uhr ablesen" row in "Nach Bereich" shows 3 tasks, 0 correct and a quote of "0 %".
