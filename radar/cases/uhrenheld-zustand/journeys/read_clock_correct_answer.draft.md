# A child reads the clock correctly and earns points

## Switch off the time limit
Click "Profil" in the bottom navigation.
In the "Einstellungen" section, untick the "Zeitdruck" checkbox so the round has no countdown.
Click "Karte" in the bottom navigation.

## Start the first station
In the station "Uhr ablesen", click "Stufe 1".
If the question says "Stelle die Uhr auf ... ein.", click "← Karte" and click "Stufe 1" under "Uhr ablesen" again.
Repeat until the question reads "Wie spät ist es? Lies die Uhr genau ab."

## Answer from the hint under the clock
The hint under the question reads like "Der blaue Stundenzeiger steht kurz nach der 3, der rote Minutenzeiger zeigt 25 Minuten an."
Type the hour number from that sentence into the "Stunde" field and the minute number into the "Minute" field.
Click "Prüfen".
Verify the feedback box starts with the text "Richtig!" followed by the points earned, e.g. "Richtig! + 10 Punkte".

## Points show up on the map
Click "← Karte".
Verify the orange header next to "Uhrenheld" shows a points count above "0 P".
