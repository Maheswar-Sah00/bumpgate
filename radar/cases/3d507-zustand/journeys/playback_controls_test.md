# Control the animation: jump to a key position and reverse the motion

## Open the Ratchet and Pawl
Click "Ratchet and Pawl" in the catalogue on the left.
Verify the details panel heading reads "Ratchet and Pawl".

## Jump to a key position in the cycle
In the animation controls at the bottom of the viewer, click the key position button labelled "End of stroke · holding pawl clicks in".
Verify the angle readout next to the timeline shows "In 180.0°" and the animation is paused (the readout stays at "In 180.0°").

## Reverse the direction
Click the reverse button in the animation controls (labelled "Reverse direction", tooltip "Reverse (R)").
Verify the note "Reverse rewinds the animation. The real wheel cannot turn backwards: the holding pawl would jam against a tooth face." appears and the speed readout shows "−1.00×".
