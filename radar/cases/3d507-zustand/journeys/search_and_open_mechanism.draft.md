# Search the catalogue and open a mechanism's details

## Check the home page loads
Wait for the page to finish loading.
Verify the catalogue on the left lists "Spur Gears" and the details panel heading reads "Spur Gears".

## Search for a movement
Click the search box with the placeholder "Search movements, #number…" and type "geneva".
Verify the catalogue list now shows only "Geneva Drive" and no longer shows "Spur Gears".

## Open the result
Click "Geneva Drive" in the catalogue list.
Verify the details panel heading reads "Geneva Drive" and the panel shows the text "Movement #212 · Ratchets & Escapements".

## Read its applications
Scroll the details panel to the "Real-world applications" section.
Verify the text "Film advance in movie projectors" is visible.
